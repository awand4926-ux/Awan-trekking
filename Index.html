About Awan Trekking

Awan Trekking is a premier local tour operator founded and based directly at the foot of Mount Batur in Kintamani, Bali. We specialize in private sunrise treks, 4WD black lava jeep safaris, and outdoor Bali adventures.

Unlike large agencies, our team consists of native Kintamani guides who grew up exploring these volcanic terrains. We prioritize your safety, comfort, and authentic cultural connection.

Hotel pick-ups start between 1:30 AM and 2:30 AM depending on your location (Ubud/Seminyak/Nusa Dua). The actual trek at the base camp starts around 3:30 AM to reach the summit right before sunrise at 6:00 AM.
We recommend wearing warm jacket/sweatshirt (it gets cool around 15°C at the summit), comfortable hiking or sport shoes, long pants, and extra cash for souvenirs or guide tip. We provide flashlights and trekking poles.
